Stay on this computer

Local-only vaults

Keep your saved data in an encrypted vault on your device, protected by a master password. No Google account required.

  • Optional OS quick unlock
  • Multiple independent vaults
  • No cloud service for local-only vaults

Keep a backup and your password. A forgotten local-only master password cannot be recovered.

↗Release validation in progress

Bring your saved workspace along

Optional Google Sync

Save selected vaults to your own Google Drive app data, including hosts, keys, passwords, snippets, and portable preferences.

  • Recovery through your Google account
  • Offline edits and conflict review
  • Separate terminal layouts for each computer

Google holds the recovery key with your encrypted data. Google account access can recover saved secrets; this is not end-to-end encryption against Google.

Google Sync is implemented; production OAuth setup and live-account recovery validation are still in progress. Live terminals, scrollback, and external files are not synced.
